# Content Idea: Article Summary Workflow

Date: 2026-06-23
Source: Recent session review found repeated article-summary prompts with strict rules around sponsor/ad removal, straight quotes, and prioritizing the most important points first.

## Draft Angle

How to turn a long online article into a useful Twitter/X thread without copying the article or wasting the reader's time.

## Why It Is Useful

Most summaries either flatten the argument or preserve too much page noise. This workflow treats summarization as a decision aid: what matters, why it matters, what changed, and which post angle is worth writing.

## Artifact To Build

A short tutorial with:
- the reusable prompt from `docs/prompt-library.md`
- before/after summary examples
- a diagram of the pipeline: article -> extraction -> claim ranking -> thread angles
- a checklist for removing page furniture, ads, and unsupported interpretation
