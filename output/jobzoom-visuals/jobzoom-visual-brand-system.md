# JobZoom Visual Brand System

## Direction
Premium executive B2B visual system for a private AI job-search operator serving senior GCC professionals.

## Visual DNA
- Dark navy / charcoal base
- Restrained gold and cyan accents
- Subtle GCC-inspired geometric patterning
- Glassmorphism product panels
- Clean sans-serif typography
- High contrast, sharp hierarchy
- Cinematic soft lighting
- Enterprise SaaS / management-consulting polish

## Content Rules
- Speak to senior professionals, not generic job seekers.
- Emphasize private daily execution: curated GCC roles, ATS-tailored CVs, daily opportunity brief, ready-to-apply package.
- Avoid guaranteed job claims, hype, fake logos, fake testimonials, and noisy debug/process labels.

## Asset Rules
- Master outputs should be 4K where possible.
- Landing hero: 3840x2160, 16:9.
- LinkedIn/feed assets: 2160x3840 for 9:16 or nearest 4K-compatible master; crop to 4:5 when needed.
- Report mockups should look like executive briefing artifacts, not internal system dashboards.
- Keep text short, readable, and spelling-perfect.
