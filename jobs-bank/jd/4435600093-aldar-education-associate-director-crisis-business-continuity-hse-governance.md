## Role purpose

Ensure Aldar Education operates safely, maintains continuity, and responds effectively to crises through a unified enterprise-wide framework. Lead the design, implementation, and governance of crisis management, business continuity, health and safety, and operational risk frameworks. Align with regulatory requirements while safeguarding students, staff, and assets and enabling uninterrupted operations across schools and corporate functions.

## Responsibilities

### Crisis management and business continuity

- Own and continuously evolve the enterprise crisis management framework, including activation, escalation, and stand-down.
- Lead crisis response and coordinate cross-functional teams during incidents.
- Develop and maintain the business continuity framework, including 24, 48, and 72-hour response readiness.
- Ensure rapid recovery and minimal disruption across schools and headquarters.

### Health, safety, and operational risk

- Oversee health, safety, and occupational health frameworks across all entities.
- Establish and govern operational risk management frameworks, including risk identification, mitigation, and monitoring.
- Ensure consistent implementation of safety standards, procedures, and controls.

### Governance, compliance, and regulatory alignment

- Establish and maintain governance frameworks across crisis, safety, and risk domains.
- Ensure compliance with relevant authorities, including ADEK, KHDA, and Civil Defence.
- Lead audit readiness, governance reviews, and compliance reporting.

### Capability building and readiness

- Lead crisis simulations, scenario planning, and readiness exercises.
- Design and deliver training to build crisis and safety capability across leadership and school teams.
- Drive a culture of safety, preparedness, and risk awareness.

### Specific duties

- Develop enterprise-wide policies, procedures, and frameworks for crisis management, business continuity, and HSE.
- Maintain decision logs, escalation protocols, and audit trails.
- Lead incident investigations and ensure timely closure of findings and actions.
- Monitor and report key risk indicators, incidents, and compliance metrics to Executive Management.
- Partner with Operations, Education, HR, and IT to embed risk, safety, and continuity practices.
- Advise Executive Leadership on crisis response, risk exposure, and resilience strategies.
- Align frameworks with Aldar Group standards and international best practice.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Risk Management, Engineering, Health and Safety, Business, or a related discipline.
- Preferred professional certification: NEBOSH Diploma, IOSH, ISO, Risk, or BCM certification.

## Experience

- 10 to 15+ years in crisis management, business continuity, risk, or health and safety.
- Proven development of enterprise governance and compliance frameworks.
- Experience in complex, multi-site environments; education or similar sectors preferred.

## Job-specific knowledge and skills

- Strong knowledge of UAE regulatory and compliance frameworks.
- Ability to lead effectively during crises and under pressure.
- Strong stakeholder management and executive communication.
- Risk governance, audit, and compliance reporting.
- High analytical, decision-making, and problem-solving capability.
- Commitment to safeguarding children and young people.

## Employer context

Aldar Education supports more than 38,000 students across 28 schools and nearly 5,000 educators representing over 100 nationalities. The group supports Abu Dhabi Economic Vision 2030 and emphasizes inclusive education, operational resilience, safety, governance, and professional development.
