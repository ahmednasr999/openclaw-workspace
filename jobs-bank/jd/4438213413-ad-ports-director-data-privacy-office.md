# AD Ports Group - Director - Data Privacy Office

- LinkedIn job ID: 4438213413
- Location: Abu Dhabi, UAE
- Work arrangement: On-site, full-time
- Application route: Oracle Cloud candidate portal, job 12099

## Job Description

Responsible for leading the design, rollout, governance, and continuous enhancement of the organization's global data privacy program, ensuring personal data is processed in compliance with GDPR and other applicable privacy laws across all jurisdictions. The role is the senior privacy leader and trusted advisor to executive management, embedding privacy by design into operations, technology, third-party relationships, and strategic initiatives while strengthening accountability, risk management, and regulatory readiness.

## Responsibilities

- Lead the enterprise-wide data privacy program, establishing strategy, governance, policies, standards, controls, and reporting.
- Serve as senior subject-matter expert on GDPR and other privacy laws and translate legal requirements into operational controls.
- Advise the Chief Compliance Officer and senior leadership on privacy risks, enforcement trends, and regulatory obligations.
- Design, implement, and mature global privacy governance forums, accountability structures, procedures, notices, and reporting mechanisms.
- Drive global rollout across business units, functions, and geographies while accommodating local legal requirements.
- Maintain records of processing activities, data inventories, retention standards, and privacy controls supporting compliance and audit readiness.
- Oversee privacy risk assessments, data protection impact assessments, transfer impact assessments, and control reviews.
- Embed privacy by design and default into projects, platforms, analytics, HR processes, customer journeys, and vendor onboarding.
- Advise on lawful bases, data subject rights, consent, retention, anonymization, cookies, profiling, and cross-border transfer mechanisms.
- Partner with Legal, Compliance, Cybersecurity, IT, Procurement, HR, Internal Audit, and business leadership.
- Monitor compliance through audits, reviews, metrics, and assurance activities.
- Lead or coordinate response to personal-data incidents and breaches, including escalation, notification support, remediation, and lessons learned.
- Engage regulators, supervisory authorities, auditors, and external counsel.
- Track global privacy regulation and enforcement and update the privacy framework.
- Build privacy capability, develop team members, strengthen awareness, and deliver targeted training.
- Provide metrics and strategic recommendations to senior leadership on program maturity and effectiveness.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Law, Information Governance, Cybersecurity, Business, Compliance, or a related discipline.
- Privacy or data-protection certifications strongly preferred, including CIPP/E, CIPP/M, CIPM, CIPT, or equivalent.
- Excellent English; Spanish and Arabic are advantages.
- Minimum 10 years in data privacy, data protection, compliance, legal, or related governance roles, including senior leadership in complex multinational organizations.
- Demonstrated experience designing and rolling out a global data privacy program.
- Strong hands-on GDPR experience, including privacy governance, DPIAs, records of processing, data subject rights, breach response, and cross-border transfer compliance.
- Executive stakeholder influence and experience with regulators, auditors, and external counsel are highly desirable.
