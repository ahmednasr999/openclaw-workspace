## Company Context
Established in 2006, Masdar, Abu Dhabi Future Energy Company, is a global clean energy leader focused on renewable energy investment, development, and operations. Masdar reports a global project portfolio capacity of more than 65 gigawatts and targets 100 gigawatts by 2030. It is jointly owned by TAQA, ADNOC, and Mubadala.

## Job Description
Masdar is seeking a visionary Enterprise Architect to join the Chief Digitalization Officer leadership team. Reporting directly to the CDO, the role serves as strategic architect and technical anchor for Masdar's global digital ecosystem. The role spearheads the Enterprise Digital function, designing a flexible, best-of-breed composable architecture that bridges modern corporate systems with agile field-level renewable asset operations.

The role owns and steers global Data Management and Data Governance strategy, defining universal data taxonomies that turn cross-border operational insight into a competitive advantage. It builds high-performing domain architects to safeguard and scale digital architecture and infrastructure, plus a robust team of data management specialists.

## Minimum Requirements
- 15+ years progressive experience operating at global, enterprise-level architecture capacity, preferably in heavily asset-intensive or energy environments.
- 10+ years directly leading, managing, and developing specialized domain architects.
- 10+ years hands-on experience in Enterprise Resource Planning, ERP, and data architecture design, with legacy-to-modern composable architecture transition.
- 8+ years architecting and managing global data management platforms.
- 5+ years driving Data Governance initiatives, including policies, frameworks, master data hierarchies, and governance bodies.
- 5+ years people management and stakeholder management.
- Strong communicator able to translate complex technical design into business language and value while influencing peers and stakeholders.
- Ability to resolve design differences and phase enterprise architecture priorities around operations and efficiency.
- Collaborative team player able to mentor and coach across the CDO team.

## Preferred Plus Factors
- SAP S/4HANA ERP Core and O&M global deployment.
- Cybersecurity and industrial architecture frameworks for critical infrastructure, such as NIST, NERC CIP, or IEC 62443.
