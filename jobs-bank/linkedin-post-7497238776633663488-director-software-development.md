The healthcare organisation is looking for an accomplished Director of Software Development to lead its software engineering function in Egypt and serve as the Egypt IT Site Leader within the Group Technology Organization.

Reporting directly to the Group CTO, this role is a key technology leadership position, acting as the CTO's representative in Egypt and ensuring that the local technology organisation operates as one connected, high-performing team.

- Own end-to-end software delivery across architecture, development, quality, release, and production support.
- Set and drive engineering standards across architecture, code quality, testing, automation, CI/CD, observability, and technical documentation.
- Drive delivery predictability, technology decisions, risk management, and engineering performance.
- Partner closely with product, clinical, and business stakeholders to turn requirements into scalable technology solutions.
- Lead the Egypt technology site, ensuring strong collaboration, engagement, operational effectiveness, and alignment with headquarters.
- Build a strong, unified engineering culture across teams and reporting lines.
- Represent Egypt Technology within Group Technology leadership forums.
