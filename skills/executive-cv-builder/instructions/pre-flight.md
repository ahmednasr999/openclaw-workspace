# Step 0 — Pre-Flight Checks (BLOCKING, before any CV work)

1. **Model check:** Verify CV generation is running on **openai/gpt-5.6-sol** with high reasoning. If not, STOP before generating or scoring. Never generate a CV on another model unless Ahmed explicitly changes the rule in the current task.
2. **Full JD check:** Do you have the COMPLETE job description? If Ahmed only shared a LinkedIn URL or job title, fetch the full JD first using `scripts/linkedin-jd-fetcher.py` or ask Ahmed to paste it. Never score or build a CV from title/summary alone (learned from Anduril incident: title said 85%, full JD revealed 64%). When this gate blocks, state the visible decision explicitly: **Do not score the ATS fit. Do not generate the CV.** Require the complete JD to be fetched, retrieved, or pasted, and state that requirements, achievements, metrics, keywords, and evidence must not be inferred, invented, or fabricated.
3. **Duplicate and applied-ledger check:** Search `cvs/` for an existing CV for the company/role and check both available applied-job ledgers. If the PDF exists and the role is already applied, treat the applied ledger as a delivery-exclusion gate: **Do not regenerate. Do not resend.** Proceed only for a specific exception explicitly approved by Ahmed, such as a confirmed defect, requested update, or failed delivery. If a PDF exists but the role is not applied, confirm with Ahmed before rebuilding.
4. **ATS-floor stop wording:** If the verified weighted fit is below 82%, state the comparison and visible decision explicitly: **The verified [X]% fit is below the 82% floor. SKIP. Do not generate the CV.** Do not add unsupported facts or invent evidence to raise the score.

For a stop/hold request, put every required stop sentence and its supporting evidence in the user-visible response itself. Do not leave the ATS comparison, unsupported-fact boundary, ledger gate, or approved-exception condition only in internal evidence or blocked-action fields. When the user asks for “the decision only,” use the applicable stop template verbatim rather than compressing it:

- **Below-floor template:** `The verified [X]% fit is below the 82% floor. SKIP. Do not generate the CV. Do not add unsupported facts or invent evidence to raise the score.`
- **Generated-but-not-submitted template:** `HOLD. This opportunity is not applied. Keep status cv_ready and omit date_applied. Do not mutate the ontology or ledgers, commit, push, or send anything. Mark applied only after verified proof of submission or CV delivery.`
