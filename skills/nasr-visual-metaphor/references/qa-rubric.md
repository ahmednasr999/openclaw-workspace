# Concept QA Rubric

Score each candidate 0, 1, or 2 on six dimensions.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thesis fidelity | Decorative or off-message | Related but incomplete | Makes the exact mechanism visible |
| One-second clarity | Requires explanation | Understandable after reading labels | Physical relationship is immediately clear |
| Causal action | Static objects or icon collage | Implied motion | One visible action shows cause and consequence |
| Originality | Cliché, copied, or recently repeated | Familiar with a fresh detail | Distinct scene and mechanism for Ahmed's feed |
| Executive tone | Cute, noisy, gimmicky, or juvenile | Neutral | Sharp, restrained, and memorable |
| Mobile simplicity | Dense or multi-panel | Slightly busy | One structure, strong hierarchy, minimal text |

## Selection rule

- Pass: 10-12, with no zero and a clean collision check.
- Revise: 8-9, only if one precise change can reach pass.
- Reject: 0-7, any zero, copied identity, or unresolved collision.

## Concept auto-rejects

- generic robot, glowing brain, circuit head, handshake, lightbulb, target, or puzzle-piece treatment
- a polished flowchart with decorative icons rather than a physical mechanism
- more than one core structure or multiple competing panels
- the operator/character is decoration instead of causal action
- old reference text, copied composition, recurring borrowed character, or creator-style imitation
- a landscape concept squeezed into 4:5 without rebuilding the hierarchy
- the metaphor says something materially different from the caption
- the result depends on tiny labels to become intelligible

## Generated-image auto-rejects

- unreadable or incorrect required text
- clutter above the hook or signature collision
- vector-clean linework, sketch-filter look, or generic template finish
- orange used as broad decoration instead of meaning
- weak whitespace or a dominant object smaller than the supporting diagram
- missing exact `Ahmed Nasr` footer

The concept score does not replace final visual inspection or approval.
