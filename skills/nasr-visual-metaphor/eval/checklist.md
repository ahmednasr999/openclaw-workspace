# NASR Visual Metaphor Output Checklist

Use before handing a concept brief to image generation.

- [ ] One evidence-bounded thesis is stated as a mechanism.
- [ ] One cognitive anchor is selected.
- [ ] Three materially different physical metaphors were considered.
- [ ] Every candidate contains one object/scene, one action, and an explicit business mapping.
- [ ] Recent visuals or visual-intent records were checked for collision, or the check is marked provisional.
- [ ] The selected concept scored at least 10/12 with no zero.
- [ ] The default composition is 4:5 portrait unless another format was requested.
- [ ] The shot list contains one dominant structure and three to five short labels.
- [ ] Total rendered copy is roughly 28 words or fewer.
- [ ] Orange carries meaning rather than decoration.
- [ ] The exact `Ahmed Nasr` footer is present.
- [ ] No borrowed mascot, copied composition, generic AI iconography, or source-style imitation appears.
- [ ] Generation, approval, scheduling, and publishing states are explicit and not conflated.

Fail closed if any required item is unresolved.
