# Content Claw Evaluation Checklist

Use this as the canonical checklist pointer for content-claw quality gates.

## Publishing safety

- Load `checklists/pre-publish.md` before approval requests, scheduling, or posting.
- Load `checklists/post-publish-verification.md` after publish or retry.

## Premium visual quality

- Load `checklists/image-post-quality.md` before saying a visual is ready.
- Also use `/root/.openclaw/workspace/docs/content-claw/premium-linkedin-visual-quality-gate.md` when deeper visual criteria are needed.

## Done means

The caption/media/source state is inspected, approval boundary is satisfied, duplicate risk is checked, and the final artifact or live state is verified.
