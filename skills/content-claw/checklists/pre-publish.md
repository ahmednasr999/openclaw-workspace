# Pre-Publish Checklist

Use before requesting approval, scheduling, or publishing.

1. Caption/draft is current and approved or explicitly requested for posting.
2. Visual/media is expected or explicitly not needed.
3. If visual expected, candidate passed image quality checklist.
4. Platform/account is confirmed.
5. No private/confidential data.
6. Claims are supportable and not overstated.
7. Caption ends with a question/CTA when appropriate.
8. Alt text is prepared where useful.
9. Duplicate check completed.
10. Approval boundary satisfied before external write.
