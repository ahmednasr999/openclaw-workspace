- **Low impact or low frequency:** monitor, but do not distract the team.

I would also validate priorities with data: complaints, NPS/CSAT, queue times, ride downtime, refunds, social media reviews, and direct guest observation.

My rule is: fix the moments that most damage trust first, especially the basics. Once the basics are stable, then invest in delight and differentiation.
