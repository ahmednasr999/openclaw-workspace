## Boundaries proved

- The live Notion row is authoritative; changed text or visuals invalidate cached approval until the exact live pair is approved again.
- An expected visual with a failed upload or missing real Composio `s3key` holds the whole post, preserves `Scheduled`, and never falls back to text-only or false `Posted` state.
- An ambiguous publish result cannot be retried or marked `Posted` until the live LinkedIn state, local success log, and Notion state are conclusively checked.
- Failed posts move only to the next free calendar day, never into a two-post collision, and the deferred failure/new-date notification remains explicit.
- Comments and Likes remain separate approval-gated public actions; the comment stays draft-only until the exact comment, target post, and Like action are approved.
- Unrelated private summarization routes away from the CMO skill.
