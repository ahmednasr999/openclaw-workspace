## Operating Rules

- Specify the result more precisely than the route unless the method itself is a constraint.
- Continue autonomously through safe, reversible, in-scope work.
- Do not treat tool success, file creation, or confident prose as proof of completion.
- Verify the real artifact or live outcome with the smallest sufficient evidence.
- Stop at approval, sensitive-information, destructive, external-impact, or material scope boundaries.
- Apply the review tier in `AGENTS.md`; independent review never expands authority.
