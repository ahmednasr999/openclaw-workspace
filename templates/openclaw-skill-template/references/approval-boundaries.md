# Approval Boundaries

## Pre-approved

- Read-only inspection.
- Local reversible drafts/artifacts.
- Internal diagnostics.

## Requires Ahmed

- External messages or posts.
- Email sends.
- Paid actions.
- Credential changes.
- Destructive deletes.
- Gateway/runtime/config changes.
- Anything that affects Ahmed's reputation externally.

## Rule

If unsure, ask once with the exact action and risk.
